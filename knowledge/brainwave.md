# BrainWave business guide

BrainWave is the discovery and insights module in the MyInnovation platform.
It helps users explore idea submissions through semantic search, theme-based
clusters, and conversational analytics.

The conversational bot can answer governed questions about submission counts,
markets, clients, themes, programs, and fiscal periods. Database facts come from
the SQL tool. Descriptive product guidance comes from this knowledge base.

In this prototype, FY26 runs from 1 April 2025 through 31 March 2026. The
configured fiscal start month is authoritative; it can be changed without
rewriting the agent.
